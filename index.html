How to play and how this game works

How to play

Stack falling blocks to complete full rows. Each completed row clears and increases your score, while the pace speeds up to keep the action intense.

  • Move with A / D or the joystick
  • Rotate with W or button 8
  • Drop with S or the stick down position
  • Hard drop with Space

About the game

Cyber Tetris was created as a cyber-themed arcade prototype using a browser-based game loop, custom UI styling, and a responsive score system designed for Raspberry Pi setups and modern web play.

It combines classic falling-block gameplay with a polished cyber security aesthetic and a live leaderboard for competitive replay.

Scoring & leaderboard

Points are awarded for line clears and higher levels. Your best score is stored once you sign in, and the leaderboard ranks players by their top result.

  • Score grows with clear count and level
  • Guest runs are local only
  • Signed-in players can save and compare scores
